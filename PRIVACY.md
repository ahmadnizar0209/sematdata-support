SematData Privacy Policy
Effective date: November 7, 2026

Your media stays on your Mac
SematData reads and edits only the files and folders you select or grant access to. Media files, artwork, file names, and metadata are processed locally. SematData does not upload this content to the developer or operate a media storage service.

No advertising or analytics
SematData does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking. It does not require a SematData account.

Purchases and the trial
Apple processes the free trial activation and optional one-time purchase through the App Store. SematData checks Apple-signed purchase records on your Mac to determine access. The developer does not receive your payment card details. The original trial activation date determines the 14-day trial period. A local Keychain record of the last observed date helps prevent clock changes from extending the trial.

Local preferences and file access
Import preferences are stored locally. File timestamps and file properties help detect changes and avoid overwriting externally modified files. Storage capacity may be checked before writing. Saving replaces the original file after verification; the app does not retain a backup copy. Keep separate backups of important media.

Data retention and deletion
SematData does not operate a developer server and does not retain your media, metadata, account details, or usage history off your Mac. Workspace state and preferences remain locally until you remove them or delete the app's data. The trial verification record remains in your Mac's Keychain so that reinstalling the app does not restart an expired trial. You can remove local SematData records through macOS or request assistance through the support page. Removing local records does not erase purchase history maintained by Apple.

Support and this website
If you choose to open a GitHub support issue, GitHub processes your account and submitted content under its own privacy policy. Issues are public, so do not post private media, passwords, payment details, or personal documents. GitHub hosts this website and may process technical connection information under its privacy policy.

Third-party protection and consent choices
SematData only relies on Apple and GitHub for the limited purposes described above. Any service provider receiving data through these flows is required to provide the same or equivalent protection described in this policy and required by applicable law. SematData has no advertising, analytics, or tracking consent to withdraw. You can edit or delete a support issue through GitHub, manage Apple purchase information through Apple, and stop future support submissions at any time.

Data control and contact
You control the original files and can remove them from the workspace without deleting them from disk. Uninstalling SematData does not delete your media. The developer has no server-side SematData data to delete. Apple manages purchase history and refund requests. For privacy questions or assistance removing local records, use the SematData support page without including sensitive information in a public issue.
